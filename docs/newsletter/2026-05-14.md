## 4. Researcher 2 Entrepreneur First Annual Showcase
**Tags:** organizations, entrepreneurship, grants, life-sciences

https://entrepreneurship.mit.edu/r2e-grant/

Researcher 2 Entrepreneur (R2E), is the Martin Trust Center's grant program with MIT HEALS for MIT PhD students and postdocs commercializing lab-born innovations in human health and the life sciences. Focused on translating some of the most essential technologies from the lab out into the world, it provides funding, entrepreneurial education, and best in class mentors.
