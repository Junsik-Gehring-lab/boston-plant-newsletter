## 1. Laura Bassi Scholarship

https://editing.press/bassi

The Laura Bassi Scholarship is an unusual non-cash award that provides editorial assistance for graduate students and early-career academics (including postdocs within five years of full-time employment).

Rather than providing research funds, the scholarship covers professional editing of theses, dissertations, and research manuscripts. It does not cover APCs or other publication costs, but can be useful if you are planning to use editorial services before submitting a thesis or paper.

In addition to the editorial support, receiving the scholarship may be a useful addition to your CV.

Note: The Bassi Foundation also offers postdoctoral research grants. Applications for the 2026 cycle are closed, but be sure to check again next year.
