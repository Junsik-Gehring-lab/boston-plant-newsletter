    Dear friends,

    There is an important update: featuring your lab on the Boston Plant Science Map, which is powered by Nico Hawley-Weld (Head of Research & Development) from Foray Bioscience.

    1. Boston Plant Science Map

    https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdNM9ou7AcV1Ej-GABoLwbPS6TFPOgK4fahUZpi7Itig4rAkQ/viewform

    Nico is in process of making a map featuring labs and companies related to plant science (or photosynthesis) in this area. This is exactly what we need - thank you again Nico!

    The map will be released at the end of this month.

    Following labs are already on the map:

    Gehring, Institute for Plant-Human Interface, Lee-Parsons, Thinking with Plants & Fungi Initiative, Voigt, Weng, Strano Research Group, Kramer, Orians, Des Marais, Shoulders, Chisholm, McRose, Sheen, Nett, Khalil

    If you can't find your lab or you know a relevant lab not on the list, please use the Google form above to update.

We will start our run/walk at 8:30am we'll return back at Area Four around 9:15am. We will mingle over coffee and pastries in the back room of Area Four.

    4. Researcher 2 Entrepreneur First Annual Showcase

    Tags: organizations, entrepreneurship, grants, life-sciences

    https://entrepreneurship.mit.edu/r2e-grant/

    Researcher 2 Entrepreneur (R2E), is the Martin Trust Center's grant program with MIT HEALS for MIT PhD students and postdocs commercializing lab-born innovations in human health and the life sciences. Focused on translating some of the most essential technologies from the lab out into the world, it provides funding, entrepreneurial education, and best in class mentors.

    Register here: https://luma.com/mpsb6wdv

    MIT HEALS: https://heals.mit.edu/

    5. Due diligence fund

    https://www.sustainable-supply-chains.org/funds-projects/due-diligence-fund/

    Supporting innovative agricultural and agrifood projects advancing environmental sustainability and human rights across global supply chains.

    Application deadline: 15 June 2026

    Eligibility:

    Open to organizations and partnerships developing innovative agricultural or agrifood solutions with measurable environmental and social impact.

    Strong partnerships and projects demonstrating impact beyond compliance are encouraged.

    Programme Goal:

    To strengthen sustainable and responsible agricultural supply chains while promoting environmental protection and social impact globally.
